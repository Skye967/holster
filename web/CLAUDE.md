## Phone-first

"What should I watch tonight" is asked from a sofa, at night, on a phone. Nothing here
is done until it works there.

- The sidebar becomes a drawer, not a column. shadcn's sidebar has a mobile mode.
- Title cards need a compact form — three posters plus metadata is a lot of scrolling.
- The input sits above the keyboard and stays there while results scroll behind it.
  This is the fiddliest part of any mobile chat UI and it is always underestimated.
- Tap targets sized for thumbs, not a mouse.
- **Design the picker phone-first.** Twenty logos with toggles is tidy on desktop and
  awful on a phone, and it is a first-run screen — the worst moment to feel clumsy.

Not a task at the end. A task at the end is the one that gets cut.
